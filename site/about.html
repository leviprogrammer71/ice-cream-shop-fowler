FAQs

Do you offer dairy-free options?
Yes — our fruit sorbets and slushies are dairy-free. Ask the team in store for the current dairy-free scoops.
Can I order ahead for pickup?
Absolutely. Place a pickup order through DoorDash and we'll have it waiting, or call (559) 400-9142 for large orders.
What is a Dubai Cup?
Our signature treat: pistachio cream, crunchy kataifi, chocolate and fresh strawberries layered in a cup. $19.49 full size, $10.39 mini.
Do you cater events and parties?
We'd love to help sweeten your event. Call or DM @theicecreamshopfresno with your date and headcount.
Do you offer delivery?
Yes — we deliver across Fresno through DoorDash. First order ships with a $0 delivery fee.
